Use this page to request deletion of data related to Mathéquête without reinstalling the app. You can also email coresrdi@gmail.com directly. No app install or account is required to use this page.

1. How to make the request

  1. On the device (optional, immediate): in Mathéquête, a parent can delete the child's profile (profile management) or uninstall the app. That removes local files, not data already sent to our servers, Stripe or Google.
  2. For data we or a partner hold: fill in the form below, or email coresrdi@gmail.com with the same information. The form opens your email app with a structured draft for you to review and send.
  3. We will process the request as soon as reasonably possible, and no later than the deadline under applicable law (in Quebec, typically 30 days).
  4. In the game (starting with the release after Play package versionCode 37), the Delete my data button in the parent area (Settings) opens this page, after a parental gate.

2. What is deleted or retained

Mathéquête does not create a child account with a password. Depending on what actually exists for you, a request may cover:

  • Deleted on request: license activations tied to your device or email, the associated device fingerprint, manual activation requests, and any school identifiers the game sent at registration (first name and, if provided, last name, level, class code).
  • On the device: you delete these yourself (profile or uninstall). We cannot reach those files remotely.
  • Google Play Games cloud save: we can help you stop further uploads. Copies already at Google are managed from the Google account (or with our help). Turning the option off in Settings does not automatically delete the Google copy.
  • Teacher account (separate Mathéquête Prof app): a teacher can delete their own account from that app. On the server, that action calls DELETE /api/prof/me/delete. A teacher may also use the form below.
  • Kept as long as the law requires: purchase invoices and receipts (Stripe or Google Play), typically several years for tax and accounting duties (Revenu Québec / Canada Revenue Agency). They will not be used for other purposes.
  • Technical logs (version checks, infrastructure traces, possibly an IP address seen by Cloudflare): these expire under the host's usual practices and our security needs. We do not promise a more specific period here.

The game does not periodically send progress statistics to a server or to the teacher app. If we hold no server-side data in your name, we will tell you.
